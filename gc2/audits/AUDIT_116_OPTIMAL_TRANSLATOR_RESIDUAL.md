# GC-II Audit 116 — Optimal-translator residual no-go

Status date: 2026-09-13

## Question
Can GC-II obtain a new invariant by comparing two realization boundaries through optimal admissible translators and retaining a nonzero residual after optimal translation?

## Setup
Let B and C be realization boundaries with semantic object sets S_B,S_C. Let T_{B→C} be an independently fixed class of admissible translators. Let d_C be a task-relevant pseudometric on C-semantics and let c(t) be a typed translator cost. For x∈S_B and target y∈S_C define

D_{B→C}(x,y) = inf_{t∈T_{B→C}} [ d_C(Sem_C(t(x)),y) + λ·c(t) ].

A symmetric residual may be built from D_{B→C} and D_{C→B}.

## Proposition 116.1 — Optimization absorption
If T, d, c and λ are fixed independently of the candidate GC quantity, then D is exactly an optimal simulation/translation objective in the chosen model. Calling D or a monotone transform of D a Generative Novelty Gap does not add mathematical content.

Proof: the displayed definition is the infimum of an independently specified loss-plus-cost objective over the independently specified feasible translator class. Any algorithm computing D is therefore solving that optimization problem. □

Status: PROVED (definitional reduction; not a novelty theorem).

## Proposition 116.2 — Zero-residual equivalence
For exact semantics (d_C(u,v)=0 iff u and v are operationally equivalent) and zero translator cost, D_{B→C}(x,y)=0 iff y lies in the operational closure of x under admissible translators, subject to attainment; without attainment, D=0 means arbitrarily accurate simulation.

Status: PROVED, with the attainment qualification explicit.

## Proposition 116.3 — Translator-class dependence
The numerical residual is not intrinsic to semantics alone. Enlarging the translator class can only decrease D; restricting it can only increase D. Rescaling the cost coordinate or λ changes D unless the corresponding units/convention are fixed externally.

Proof: infimum monotonicity under set inclusion; cost rescaling is immediate. □

Status: PROVED.

## Consequence for Omega_G
A residual of the form

Omega_G = inf_translator (semantic error + typed realization burden)

is not a representation-independent GC-II invariant merely because the infimum is taken over optimal translators. Its content resides in the independently chosen translator class, semantic loss, and cost geometry. It collides structurally with simulation preorders, resource conversion optimization, compiler/simulation overhead, and (for statistical experiments) deficiency/randomization distances.

In particular, Le Cam deficiency already compares experiments by optimizing over randomizations/Markov kernels; resource theories characterize convertibility and rates by allowed operations and monotones. Therefore 'optimize over translators, then call the residual capability novelty' is rejected as a standalone breakthrough route.

Status: FALSIFIED AS STANDALONE NOVELTY.

## What survives
A potentially nontrivial GC-II target must not be just another optimized translator distance. The remaining candidate is a *translator obstruction generated by composition of several independently meaningful boundaries*: prove that every pairwise translator can have zero (or bounded) residual while a jointly compatible translator family is impossible or incurs a strictly positive excess burden.

Candidate object (OPEN): for boundaries B_1,...,B_k with independently specified pairwise translator constraints, define a compatibility defect only after quotienting by semantic equivalence and translator refactorings. The key test is whether the joint defect is reducible to known contextuality/sheaf obstruction, marginal consistency, database join/decomposability, CSP consistency/width, cocycle obstruction, distributed agreement, or multi-resource convertibility.

A breakthrough claim is prohibited until that collision gate is passed and a quantitative operational consequence is independently derived.

## Edge/degenerate checks
- Identity boundary with identity translator: residual 0.
- Empty translator class: +∞; not evidence of novelty.
- Universal free translator: semantic residual can collapse to 0; demonstrates boundary dependence.
- Duplicate/refactored translator descriptions: infimum unchanged when semantics and costs are preserved.
- Enlarging translator class: residual nonincreasing.
- Composition: no triangle inequality follows unless translator classes compose and costs/loss obey compatible subadditivity; do not assume a metric.
- Typed costs: scalarization λ·c requires dimensionless weights or an explicitly fixed exchange convention. Without it, cross-resource addition is dimensionally unjustified.

## Prior-art collision status
- simulation/convertibility preorder: COLLISION
- resource-theory conversion rates/monotones: COLLISION
- Le Cam/Blackwell comparison via randomization/deficiency: COLLISION for statistical boundaries
- compiler/simulation overhead: COLLISION in computational realizations
- joint compatibility/contextual obstruction: OPEN collision gate

## Decision
Decisive falsification: optimal pairwise translation residual, even quotient-stable within a fixed realization convention, is not enough for GC-II novelty. Advance next to a joint-compatibility/excess-burden construction and try to kill it against contextuality, marginal problems, database decomposability, CSP consistency, cocycles, and distributed agreement before promoting it.
