# Literature watch — 2026-09-08

Branch-only research note; this is not a novelty claim.

## Material prior-art constraints checked

1. Hsieh & Gessner, *Complete Characterization of State Conversions by Work Extraction*, Physical Review Letters 137, 070403 (published 2026-08-12; DOI 10.1103/4f1s-5q8b). Necessary-and-sufficient task-based characterization of state conversion in general quantum resource theories. Consequence for GC: do not claim novelty for universal task-based resource certification, conversion criteria, or translation cost by themselves.
2. Siddiqui & Wang, *Operational Concealment of Measurement Incompatibility by Quantum Channels*, arXiv:2607.11762 (2026-07-13). Introduces operational equivalence classes induced by an adjoint-channel kernel and quantitative concealment robustness. Consequence for GC: quotienting by operational indistinguishability/equivalence is not itself novel; any GC translation-spectrum invariant must depend on genuinely whole-envelope structure beyond such quotient constructions.
3. Catani, Galley & Gonda, *Resource-theoretic hierarchy of contextuality for general probabilistic theories*, Quantum 10, 2077 (2026-04-21). Consequence for GC: GPT/resource-theoretic hierarchy and simulation structure remain strong neighboring prior art.
4. Recent 2026 resource-theory work on cross-resource characterization and asymptotic conversion further reinforces that multi-resource characterization, state-conversion rates, and resource-theoretic equivalence are not sufficient novelty targets on their own.
5. Edalatpanah, *The Awareness Logic of Ambiguity (ALA): Triadic Interpretive States and Their Structure-Preserving Operational Core*, arXiv:2608.15093 (submitted 2026-08-15). Its Non-Collapse Theorem gives a direct conceptual precedent for projection loss: states equal after an admissible scalar projection can become distinguishable under a structure-preserving operation. Consequence for GC: loss of operational information under projection, and the slogan that projection equality need not imply operational equivalence, cannot be claimed as GC-native novelty. GC's Strong Proper-Projection Irreducibility remains mathematically valid, but novelty must rest on the stronger all-strict-coordinate-projections parity construction in the embodied task–scale–error–budget setting, its unbounded projection order, or a genuinely quantitative whole-envelope consequence.
6. Waseem, *String Diagrams for Quantum Foundations, Computing and Natural Language Processing*, arXiv:2605.11417 (submitted 2026-05-12). The thesis formalises constructor theory as a process theory using symmetric monoidal categorical structure and explicitly studies composition/locality constraints. Consequence for GC: merely adding serial/parallel composition or requiring composition-preserving translations is not enough for novelty; a GC-native composition result must exploit additional envelope structure such as joint task/scale/error/resource feasibility or reachable-state dependence.
7. Marletto, Deutsch & Vedral, *Tests of constructor theory*, arXiv:2606.07352 (submitted 2026-06-05). Constructor theory is presented as imposing task-possibility principles with experimentally discriminating predictions. Consequence for GC: a claimed physical breakthrough must supply a genuinely new quantitative prediction or falsification criterion beyond task-possibility language itself.
8. Rosenthal, *Prior-free Blackwell*, Economic Theory Bulletin 14, article 4 (published 2026-03-17). Extends comparison-of-experiments analysis to a prior-free robust decision criterion. Consequence for GC: variants of information-order comparison remain active and broad; GC should avoid novelty claims based on modifying the decision criterion while retaining experiment simulability as the core object.

## Novelty assessment after this watch

- Whole embodied task–scale–error–budget–composition envelope: OPEN as a historical-priority claim; no direct collision established in this watch.
- Strong proper-projection irreducibility: PROVED mathematically in the repository, but its parity skeleton is classical and projection non-collapse now has an additional explicit 2026 operational precedent; novelty must be scoped to the GC formulation/application and any stronger all-projection consequence.
- Translation-spectrum invariant beyond known simulation/resource orders: OPEN. The operational-concealment literature raises the bar and rules out novelty based merely on quotienting by operational indistinguishability. Process-theory prior art also rules out novelty from composition preservation alone.
- Universal scalable-envelope conservation: FALSIFIED by the repository's cyclic reversal construction.
- Same-input whole-envelope quantitative prediction beyond neighboring frameworks: OPEN.

## Breakthrough decision

No validated breakthrough in this literature pass. No theorem status is upgraded. The strongest research route remains a GC-native invariant or impossibility theorem that distinguishes worlds with matching established state/channel/resource/GPT projections and ordinary process-composition invariants, ideally with a quantitative same-input consequence. The ALA collision means that a future breakthrough cannot consist merely of showing information loss under a projection; the process-theory collision likewise means it cannot consist merely of enforcing composition preservation. It must exploit the complete embodied envelope or establish a stronger quantitative separation.
