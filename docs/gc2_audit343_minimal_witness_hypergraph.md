# GC-II Audit 343 — Minimal-witness hypergraph interaction calculus

## Scope
This audit attacks the compression problem left open by Audit 342. GC-I on `main` is untouched.

Let `B` be a finite baseline reachability preorder on `Q`, let `E` be a finite set of quotient-deduplicated newly grounded transitions, and for each ordered pair `p=(x,y)` define the monotone Boolean reachability function

`g_p(S)=1[p ∈ TC(B ∪ S) \ B]`, for `S⊆E`.

Then `f_B(S)=Σ_p g_p(S)` and Audit 342's interaction coefficient is `J_B(T)=Σ_{U⊆T}(-1)^(|T|-|U|) f_B(U)`.

For each pair `p`, let `W_p` be the antichain of inclusion-minimal subsets `W⊆E` such that `g_p(W)=1`. Call these the minimal new-capability witnesses for `p`.

## Theorem 343.1 — Witness-hypergraph representation [PROVED]
For every `S⊆E`,

`g_p(S)=1  iff  ∃ W∈W_p with W⊆S`.

Equivalently, `g_p` is the monotone DNF whose prime positive terms are exactly the minimal witnesses `W_p`.

Proof: upward closure follows from monotonicity of transitive closure under edge addition. In a finite ground set, every successful `S` contains an inclusion-minimal successful subset. Conversely any superset of a successful witness remains successful.

## Theorem 343.2 — Exact union-of-minimal-witness formula [PROVED]
Let `j_p(T)` be the Boolean-lattice Möbius coefficient of `g_p`. Then for every nonempty `T⊆E`,

`j_p(T) = Σ_{∅≠K⊆W_p : ⋃_{W∈K} W = T} (-1)^(|K|+1)`.

Therefore

`J_B(T)=Σ_p j_p(T)`

and

`Omega_pair(E|B)=Σ_p Σ_{∅≠K⊆W_p} (-1)^(|K|+1)` evaluated through the corresponding witness unions (equivalently `Σ_{∅≠T⊆E}J_B(T)`).

Proof: `g_p(S)` is the indicator of the union of events `{W⊆S}` over `W∈W_p`. Inclusion-exclusion gives

`g_p(S)=Σ_{∅≠K⊆W_p}(-1)^(|K|+1) 1[⋃K⊆S]`.

The Möbius transform of the principal-upset indicator `1[T⊆S]` is the point mass at `T`; collecting equal unions proves the formula. Summing over `p` commutes with the finite Möbius transform.

## Corollary 343.3 — Structural support criterion [PROVED]
A global interaction coefficient can be nonzero only at a set `T` that is a union of minimal witnesses for at least one ordered pair. Thus Audit 342's nominal `2^|E|-1` interaction table can be replaced exactly by the union-closure support generated by the pairwise minimal-witness hypergraphs, followed by cancellation across pairs.

This is a structural compression, not a promise of polynomial size: the witness family and its union closure can themselves be exponential.

## Corollary 343.4 — Unique-witness sparsity [PROVED]
If every newly reachable ordered pair has a unique minimal witness `W_p`, then `j_p(T)=1[T=W_p]`. Hence

`J_B(T)=#{p : W_p=T}` ≥ 0,

and all other coefficients vanish. In this regime the exact interaction spectrum is sparse and nonnegative even though witness size can be arbitrarily high.

Therefore negative interaction coefficients require alternative minimal witnesses/overlap structure somewhere in the system; they cannot arise in the unique-minimal-witness regime.

## Corollary 343.5 — Interaction-order bound from witness-union rank [PROVED]
If, for every pair `p`, every union of subfamilies of `W_p` has cardinality at most `d`, then `J_B(T)=0` for `|T|>d`. A simpler sufficient condition is `|⋃_{W∈W_p}W|≤d` for every `p`.

This supplies an explicit structural condition under which Audit 342's high-order expansion truncates exactly. It is sufficient, not necessary, because cancellations can create additional zeros.

## Edge and invariance checks
- `E=∅`: no nonempty coefficients; novelty is zero.
- Pair already in `B`: `W_p=∅` and contributes nothing.
- Duplicate syntax for one grounded transition must be quotient-deduplicated before `E` is formed.
- Relabelling states or edge labels only relabels witness hypergraphs; coefficient values are invariant.
- Cycles and non-antisymmetric baselines are allowed.
- Minimal witnesses are semantic: they depend on baseline closure and grounded transition meaning, not rule names.

## Exact finite verification
`experiments/gc2_audit343_minimal_witness_verifier.py` enumerates all labelled preorders on three states, all absent grounded-edge universes, every subset, every newly reachable pair, its minimal witnesses, direct Möbius coefficients, and the witness-union prediction. It also checks the unique-witness corollary. The recorded run covers 29 preorders and 473 subset coefficients with zero formula failures.

## Prior-art collision status
Minimal path sets plus inclusion-exclusion are classical in network reliability, and monotone Boolean functions/minimal true sets and Boolean-lattice Möbius inversion are established machinery. Accordingly the witness-DNF and inclusion-exclusion mechanism is **IMPORTED/KNOWN**. The GC-II contribution claimed here is narrower: an exact translation of Audit 342's capability-interaction coefficients into minimal operational witness hypergraphs, exposing a concrete sufficient condition for exact interaction-order truncation and a semantic compression target. No novelty is claimed for minimal-path or Möbius theory itself.
