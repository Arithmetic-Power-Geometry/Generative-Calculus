# GC-II Audit 372 — coalition interaction is Möbius/Harsanyi structure, not a new novelty invariant

## Scope
Branch-only Paper-II audit. GC-I/main is unchanged.

## Setup
Let N be a finite set of operational components. Fix an initial state, target capability, admissibility rules, and a budget. For each coalition S⊆N define a capability score v(S)∈R (binary reachability v∈{0,1} is the minimal case).

A natural attempt after Audit 371 is to call the capability available only under joint composition a generative interaction or novelty term.

## Exact decomposition
Define
[
delta(T)=sum_{Usubseteq T}(-1)^{|T|-|U|}v(U).
]
Then Möbius inversion on the Boolean lattice gives
[
v(S)=sum_{Tsubseteq S}delta(T)
]
for every S⊆N, uniquely.

For two components,
[
delta({1,2})=v(12)-v(1)-v(2)+v(arnothing).
]
Thus the obvious nonadditive residual is exactly the second-order Möbius coefficient / Harsanyi dividend. Higher-order inclusion-exclusion residuals are the corresponding higher-order coefficients.

### Status
- Exact coalition decomposition: **PROVED / IMPORTED-KNOWN**.
- Uniqueness: **PROVED / IMPORTED-KNOWN**.
- Raw interaction residual as a GC-II foundational invariant: **FALSIFIED**.

## Superactivation collision
If v(∅)=v(1)=v(2)=0 and v(12)=1, then δ(12)=1. This is the abstract pattern “two individually zero-capability objects become capable jointly.” The pattern is not novel: activation/superactivation is established in resource and quantum-information settings. Therefore existence of positive joint escape alone cannot support a GC-II novelty claim.

## Sign counterexample
Even monotone capability scores do not force Möbius interaction coefficients to be nonnegative. Let
v(∅)=0, v(1)=v(2)=v(12)=1.
This is monotone, but
[
delta(12)=1-1-1+0=-1.
]
Hence a Möbius/Harsanyi coefficient cannot generically be interpreted as a nonnegative “amount of generated novelty” without extra structural assumptions.

Likewise, for an OR-type n-component reachability function, higher-order coefficients alternate in sign. Monotonicity of capability therefore does not imply positivity of pure interaction.

### Status
- “Monotone capability ⇒ nonnegative interaction novelty”: **FALSIFIED** by the 2-component example.
- “Positive pair interaction = specifically GC generativity”: **FALSIFIED** as a general claim by prior-art collision.

## What survives
A defensible GC-II interaction quantity must use more than the terminal coalition set function v. It must depend on operationally meaningful budget/resource/information/interface structure while remaining invariant under conservative presentation changes. It must also prove a relation to capability cost/escape that is not obtainable by simply Möbius-transforming coalition values.

A possible surviving target is a *budget-indexed interaction frontier*: compare Pareto-minimal resource vectors needed by S with those generated by proper subcoalitions. But this is only a target; it must be collision-tested against multiobjective optimization, cooperative games, activation/resource theories, and network synthesis before any novelty claim.

## Prior-art collision notes
1. Harsanyi dividends are precisely the Möbius inversion of a cooperative-game characteristic function and isolate coalition surplus beyond proper subcoalitions.
2. Möbius inversion on the Boolean lattice is the standard inclusion-exclusion decomposition of set functions.
3. Superactivation is established: objects/channels with zero capacity individually can have positive capacity jointly.

## Consequence for Paper II
Do not use a scalar residual
[
J(S)=v(S)-	ext{(lower-order coalition terms)}
]
as the central GC-II breakthrough unless additional GC-native structure changes the theorem. The unrestricted construction is classical, and even monotone reachability does not give it the positivity needed for a generic novelty measure.
