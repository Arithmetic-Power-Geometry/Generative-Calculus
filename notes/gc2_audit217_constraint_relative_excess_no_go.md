# GC-II Audit 217 — Constraint-relative compilation excess is not intrinsic

Status: **PROVED no-go / prior-art boundary**

Starting branch head: `2bf5a40cdab7348daf6fe338ee5ea60fc03ec427`.

## Candidate under test

Audit 216 left the constrained compilation excess

\[
\Xi_{\mathcal K}(H)=\log_2 M_{\min}^{\mathcal K}(H)-\log_2 N(H)\ge 0,
\]

where `N(H)` is the unrestricted minimal behavioral realization size and
`M_min^K(H)` is the minimum exact simulator size within an independently
specified implementation class `K` (local, distributed, modular-interface,
online, etc.).

## Theorem 217.1 — Constraint-choice no-go

Fix any finite behavior `H` with finite unrestricted minimal realization
`N(H)`. The quantity `Xi_K(H)` is not an intrinsic invariant of `H` unless the
constraint class `K` is itself canonically determined by additional
operational structure.

### Proof

Let `K_all` contain all exact deterministic realizations. By minimality,

\[
M_{\min}^{\mathcal K_{all}}(H)=N(H),\qquad \Xi_{\mathcal K_{all}}(H)=0.
\]

Now choose any `m>N(H)` and define `K_m` to contain exact realizations with at
least `m` states (padding by unreachable or behaviorally redundant states is
allowed). An exact padded realization with exactly `m` states exists, so

\[
M_{\min}^{\mathcal K_m}(H)=m,
\qquad
\Xi_{\mathcal K_m}(H)=\log_2(m/N(H)).
\]

As `m` is arbitrary, the same external behavior has constraint-relative excess
ranging from zero to arbitrarily large finite values. If `K_empty` contains no
exact realization of `H`, the constrained minimum is infinite. Therefore
`Xi_K` cannot be intrinsic to behavior or unrestricted generative closure.
QED.

The padding witness is deliberately adversarial: it proves that *some* external
restriction is mathematically necessary, not that artificial padding is a
scientifically useful restriction.

## Corollary 217.2 — A constrained lower bound needs a model certificate

Any claim `Xi_K(H)>0` must identify operational facts that select `K`
independently of the theorem being proved (e.g. a measured network topology,
fixed interface partition, bounded message alphabet, locality radius, or
online-information restriction). Otherwise the excess can be manufactured by
model choice.

## Prior-art collision boundary

Once `K` is fixed independently, lower bounds on simulation blow-up are a
classical descriptional/state-complexity question. Restricted automata can
require exponential or larger simulation blow-ups in other automaton models;
distributed models similarly obtain communication/message lower bounds from
explicit network and communication restrictions. Thus `constraint-induced
simulation overhead` alone is not GC-II novelty.

## Consequence for GC-II

The surviving route is **not** to optimize over an arbitrarily declared `K`.
GC-II needs a constraint generated by the *same operational datum* that defines
the local views/projections in GC-I, and then must prove that the resulting
translator lower bound is invariant under semantics-preserving re-encodings
that respect that datum.

A defensible next object is therefore a *certified projection architecture*
`P=(X,{pi_i},I)` supplied as part of the operational system, with translators
restricted only by preservation of the measured interfaces `pi_i`, rather than
by a hand-picked machine class. The open question is whether proper-projection
irreducibility forces a positive excess after quotienting out ordinary
behavioral minimization and whether that excess escapes database/CSP,
communication-complexity, and marginal/contextuality reductions.

## Ledger

- `Xi_K >= 0` when `K` is a subclass of unrestricted exact realizations: **PROVED**.
- `Xi_K` intrinsic without a canonical/operationally certified `K`: **FALSIFIED**.
- Arbitrarily large finite `Xi_K` for fixed behavior by changing `K`: **PROVED**.
- Infinite `Xi_K` when the constrained class cannot realize the behavior: **PROVED**.
- Restricted-model simulation/state blow-up as a general mechanism: **IMPORTED/KNOWN**.
- Projection-architecture-derived translator excess invariant: **OPEN**.

No claim of novelty is made for the no-go mechanism itself.
